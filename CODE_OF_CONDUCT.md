## Our promise

The Scyther repository is a place to do careful work, ask honest questions, and help one
another learn. We welcome people of every background, identity, experience
level, nationality, and point of view. There is room here for serious debate,
plain language, a little good humor, and the understanding that everyone has
something worth contributing.

This policy is inspired by Dolly Parton. It is not written by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Dolly Parton or her organizations.

## Project focus

Scyther remains open and minimally maintained for now (but with very limited resources) to support educators and existing users;
no major new features are planned. As also mentioned in the manual: For new, state-of-the-art research projects,
we strongly recommend the [Tamarin Prover](https://tamarin-prover.com/) instead
of Scyther.

## How we show up

In project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and
other communications, we will:

- Treat people with respect, even when we disagree about code, research, or
  process.
- Be generous with newcomers and make technical criticism specific, relevant,
  and constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and use language that lets others
  participate safely and fully.

## What does not belong here

We do not accept harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, doxxing, stalking, or hateful language. This
applies to conduct based on any personal characteristic, including age,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, race,
religion, sexual orientation, or technical experience.

Bad-faith posting is also unacceptable. This includes deliberately derailing
discussion, repeated provocation, knowingly false claims about people or their
work, spam, sockpuppeting, or using project spaces to intimidate or exhaust
others. Disagreement is welcome; disruption is not.

Submitting generic, inaccurate, deceptive, or unreviewed AI-generated content
is bad-faith posting. Maintainers may remove it without debate. A contributor
who continues after being asked to stop may lose access to project spaces,
temporarily or permanently.

## Reporting concerns

Please report conduct that may violate this policy privately to a repository
maintainer using the maintainer contact information published with the project.
Include links, screenshots, dates, and any other information that can help us
understand what happened. Do not feel obliged to confront the person involved.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report as soon as reasonably possible, handle it
with appropriate discretion, and share details only with people who need them
to respond. Reports made maliciously or in bad faith may themselves be treated
as a conduct violation.

## How concerns are handled

Maintainers will review the available context, consider the impact on the
people involved and the community, and choose a response proportionate to the
conduct. Responses may include a private warning, a request to edit or remove
content, moderation of comments, a temporary participation restriction, or a
permanent ban from project spaces.

Harassment and bad-faith posting will be acted on, not waved away as just part
of open-source discussion. Urgent threats or immediate safety concerns may be
reported to the relevant platform or local emergency services.

## Scope

This policy applies in all Scyther project spaces. We ask everyone to help keep this a
welcoming place to build, question, and learn.
